# Marina Bay Sands — original game model

This is original geometry authored for Wobble Heads, based on public architectural references. It is not a downloaded scan, official asset, precise survey, or complete reconstruction of the integrated resort. It represents the existing three-tower hotel and SkyPark; the wider resort, interiors and future development are omitted. Base paving and planting are simplified.

References:
- [Safdie Architects: Hotel and SkyPark](https://www.safdiearchitects.com/projects/marina-bay-sands-hotel-and-skypark): paired tower plates, splayed atriums, façade character and rooftop amenities.
- [Arup: Marina Bay Sands](https://www.arup.com/projects/marina-bay-sands-integrated-resort/): 340 m by 38 m SkyPark proportions.
- [Marina Bay Sands: Architecture](https://www.marinabaysands.com/guides/exceptional-experiences/marina-bay-sands-architecture.html): three towers, rooftop pool and observation deck.

No reference photographs or third-party model files are distributed in this asset. Source implementation: `prototype/js/marina-bay-sands.js`. Rebuild standalone GLB with `node scripts/export-marina-bay-sands.mjs`. Metadata records its geometry and byte budget. World units are approximate metres; +Z faces the bay.
