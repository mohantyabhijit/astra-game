Drive the 2026 Marina Bay circuit and collect purple gems. Every three gems starts a pursuit; H starts one immediately. Follow the amber road route to the nearest reachable cooldown area. Enter at the P marker, park behind the opaque screen, and stay unseen for 8 seconds. Leaving cover or being spotted interrupts cooldown. Cleared heat resumes collection. Gems save automatically. Vehicle theft is deferred: you already have the car.
